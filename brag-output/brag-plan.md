# Brag Plan: Kibou

## What is this app?
Kibou is an anonymous peer mental-health platform for students — pick a topic, get matched with a peer helper or the Sukhi AI companion in seconds, with encrypted chat, crisis escalation, and counselor moderation.

## The angle
The 2am test: it's late, you're spiraling about exams, and there's nowhere to say it out loud. Kibou answers in seconds — no name, no account, no waiting room. The video is one stressed student going from alone to heard in 20 seconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant type on near-black: "It's 2am. Your brain won't shut up." — then the word "Kibou" resolves in safe-green. Earns the next 17 seconds because every student has lived that night.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Topic pills + a cursor tapping "Find Peer Helper" — the product's front door, real copy.
- Chat bubbles arriving one by one, ending in Sukhi's particle orb "reflecting" — the signature visual.
- The trust stack: "100% Anonymous" → "Tele-MANAS 14416 on every crisis" → helper toast "Someone needs support".

## Outro / punchline
Kibou wordmark + "No name. No waiting. Just someone there." Music fades, one soft bell.

## User flow worth showing
Pick a topic (Academic Stress) → tap Find Peer Helper → chat bubbles flow, Sukhi reflects with the orb → support lands, escalation safety net visible.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: warm good-vibes launch for a campus kindness app
- Interpretation: playful and clean but never jokey about the subject — high motion energy, sincere copy, restraint on SFX. Pacing comfortable, holds long enough to read every line.

## Format: landscape — 1280x720
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #121212
- Accent: #16a34a (safe green)
- Text: #ffffff
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts, from index.html); fallback system sans in render
- Body font: system sans (-apple-system stack)
- Strongest visual element: the thinking-orb particle shimmer + dark chat bubbles with green accents

## Share copy (draft)
Kibou — anonymous peer support for students. No name, no account, no waiting. Vent to a peer or Sukhi AI, 24/7.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (1:54, mid-energy laid-back, ~115 BPM)
- Music treatment: in at 0s, bed at 0.3–0.4, fade out over final 2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `.agents/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; strong cues at 3.70, 4.23, 6.34, 10.54, 12.65s; beat grid ~0.52s spacing — snap sequential text to every other beat or hold the full set
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; RMS breathes the green glow behind the orb and logo, nothing else
- SFX posture: moderate; motion-matched, professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: hook type-in (key ticks), Find Peer Helper tap (click), chat bubbles arriving (soft drops), toast slide-in (plate ping), outro logo (soft bell)
- Restraint rule: no SFX under readable sentences; never score every bubble — accent arrivals only

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Dark #121212. "It's 2am." slams in, then "Your brain won't shut up." underneath. Beat: the word "Kibou" in #16a34a resolves over it all at ~2.2s.
Sequential/interaction: two lines arrive one by one, then the brand resolves; or none beyond that.
Audio intent: music starts under; curiosity, intimacy.
Audio-coupled idea: typed/key-tick texture on the hook lines if a typing animation is used.
Music: vol-9 bed, low at open.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Front door — 4s
Phone-frame mock of the seeker dashboard: topic pills (Academic Stress, Anxiety & Panic, Relationships) pop in, then a cursor taps the "Find Peer Helper" button (real copy). Button flashes green.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 pills arrive one by one (~0.5s apart, held as a set), then simulated cursor tap on the button.
Audio intent: momentum, "this is easy".
Audio-coupled idea: soft drops for pills, click for the tap.
Music: bed lifts slightly.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The conversation — 6s
Chat view: seeker bubble "exams tomorrow and I'm spiraling", then Sukhi's particle orb shimmers with "Sukhi is reflecting…", then Sukhi's reply bubble lands: "That night-before panic is real. What's the one chapter scaring you most?" Peer helper toast slides in at the edge: "Someone needs support".
Sequential/interaction: yes — bubbles arrive one by one and hold; orb shimmers between them.
Audio intent: warmth arriving; the emotional core.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop per bubble arrival (thin: first and last only), subtle shimmer bed under orb.
Music: bed steady; beat-lock the reply landing near 10.54s strong cue if natural.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Trust stack — 4s
Three lines land one by one, held as a set: "100% Anonymous. No account." / "Encrypted end to end." / "Tele-MANAS 14416 on every crisis." Real claims, real number.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 lines, snapped to every other beat, full set held ≥1.5s.
Audio intent: reassurance, weight.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop per line; nothing under the helpline number — let it sit.
Music: bed steady.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
Kibou wordmark centers with green glow breathing. "No name. No waiting. Just someone there." Music fades over the last 2s, one soft bell as the logo lands.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: landing, warmth.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo bell.
Music: fade out 18–20s.
Transition mood: end.
